# Neartropolitan messaging

Draft positioning. Product capabilities remain proposed until demonstrated.

## Audience and promise

For players who enjoy city builders and trading, Neartropolitan is a city where businesses depend on one another and your decisions affect what happens next.

Suggested headline: "Build your business. Change your city."

Supporting copy: "Grow crops, supply local shops, deliver orders, and reinvest in a neighborhood shaped by its residents."

The first demo must say "Testnet prototype" near balances and payments. Never imply that testnet balances can be cashed out.

## Signature story

The bakery needs wheat. You can supply it, wait for a better offer, or start a competing business. Residents have budgets, and your decisions change what they can buy. Use only choices that exist in the current build in published copy.

## Proof

Show a shortage, the player's response, the resident purchase, and the completed settlement. Show that a cart changes delivery capacity. Explain where resident money comes from.

## Interface language

Building actions: "Visit market", "Harvest wheat", "Review sale", and "Accept delivery". Pending state: "Trade submitted. Waiting for confirmation." Budget state: "This resident cannot afford that offer." Bank label: "Transaction history".

## Claim boundaries

Avoid passive-income promises, guaranteed earnings, investment language, and claims that every interaction is a transaction. Navigation is immediate; economic settlement uses NEAR. Say "resident rules" rather than "AI citizens" until AI materially participates. Private bank transfers are future work and must not appear as an available capability.

## Messaging validation

Ask players what they expect to do in the first minute. If they describe a wallet dashboard instead of a game, revise the presentation. Ask why a cart is useful and why a resident stops buying. Their answers should reflect the implemented rules.

## Writing skills

Use `technical-writing` for clarity and consistent terminology, then `unslop` to remove filler and unsupported claims. Neither is a dedicated positioning-research skill. Use `hackathon-strategist` to check that claims match track fit and demonstrated differentiation.
