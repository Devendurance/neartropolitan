# Neartropolitan product idea

Neartropolitan is a navigable city game where players build businesses, trade useful goods, and change the local economy through their decisions.

## User and appeal

The first player enjoys city builders, production chains, and market decisions. The experience should remain understandable without blockchain knowledge. Buildings are places to act: grow at the farm, trade at the market, produce at the bakery, and inspect money at the bank.

## Core loop

Grow wheat, sell it to a bakery, produce bread, deliver it to residents, and use proceeds to improve capacity. Decide whether to sell now, store goods, buy a delivery cart, or expand production. Assets matter because they change production or delivery, not merely because they have a token price.

## Signature mechanic

Residents have finite budgets, recurring needs, and price preferences. A shortage changes demand and offers. A player's sale can restore bread production and enable deliveries. Every economic outcome has a visible cause.

## Differentiation

The official directory includes Trading Town, NPC-Town, Vibe City, and Farmverse. Their implementations have not been audited. A city theme alone is not a defensible distinction. Prove a closed production chain with budget-constrained residents and a player decision that changes its outcome.

## Track fit and scope

Games With Real Economies emphasizes the game loop, NPC wallets, and player markets. Build one neighborhood with a farm, bakery, market, delivery depot, and bank. Keep a larger navigable city as the product direction. Private transfers, cars beyond one functional cart, land speculation, and financial yield are deferred.

The proposed economy uses testnet funds for its first demonstration. Crop harvests create goods, not guaranteed financial returns. Resident spending comes from explicitly funded, finite treasuries. Long-term demand remains a design question requiring playtests.
